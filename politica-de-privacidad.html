At Sucesso, we are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring the security of the personal information you provide to us. This policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your data when you visit our website.

1. Data Controller

Sucesso is a registered clothing brand in Venezuela. For any inquiries about this policy, you can write to: info@sucessobrand.com.

2. Data We Collect

When browsing or shopping on our website, we may collect the following data:

  • Name and surname
  • Shipping address
  • Email address
  • Phone number
  • Payment information (securely processed by payment providers)
  • Browsing data (cookies, IP address, browser type)

3. Purpose of Data

We use your data to:

  • Process and deliver your orders
  • Contact you if necessary (order confirmation, support, promotions if you have given consent)
  • Improve our website and your user experience
  • Comply with our legal obligations

4. Use of Cookies

Our site uses cookies to facilitate navigation and personalize your experience. You can set your browser to reject cookies, although this may affect some site features.

5. Information Security

We implement technical and organizational measures to protect your data against unauthorized access, loss, or alteration.

6. Data Sharing

We do not sell or transfer your personal data to third parties except when necessary to:

  • Process payment (secure payment platforms)
  • Handle delivery (courier companies)
  • Comply with legal obligations

7. User Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access your personal data
  • Request the correction of inaccurate data
  • Request deletion of your data when no longer needed or consent is withdrawn
  • Object to data processing for commercial purposes

You can exercise these rights by writing to help.sucsso@gmail.com.

8. Policy Changes

We may update this policy to adapt it to legal changes or site operations. We will publish changes on this page.

9. Contact

For any questions about this policy or your personal data, contact us at:

Email: info@sucessobrand.com
